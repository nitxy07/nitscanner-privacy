NIT Scanner Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

NIT Scanner ("we", "our", or "the App") is an Android application that allows users to scan documents, pages, books, ID cards, and QR codes.

1. Information We Collect

NIT Scanner does not knowingly collect or sell personal information from users. We do not operate our own servers, and your scans are not sent to us.

The App may process images captured through the device camera to provide document scanning, ID card scanning, and QR code scanning functionality.

Some services from Google that are built into the App (advertising, purchases, and optional Google Drive backup) do collect or process limited data. These are explained in sections 6, 7 and 8 below.

2. Camera Permission

NIT Scanner requires access to your device camera to scan documents, books, ID cards, and QR codes.

Camera access is used only to provide the scanning functionality of the App.

3. Google ML Kit

NIT Scanner uses Google ML Kit technologies to provide scanning and recognition features (document scanning, text recognition, and QR / barcode reading). This processing happens on your device.

Information processed by Google ML Kit is handled according to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms. Google may collect limited diagnostic and usage information about these features.

4. Scanned Documents

Your scanned documents are processed and stored locally on your device, in the App's private storage.

NIT Scanner does not upload your scanned documents to our own servers. Scans leave your device only when you choose to: for example when you share a file with another app, save an export to your device's Pictures or Downloads folder, or turn on Google Drive backup (see section 8).

If Android's device backup is turned on in your phone settings, App data may be included in the backup that Android stores in your own Google account.

5. Personal Information

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, or physical address through the App.

If you choose to sign in with your Google account for Google Drive backup, the App shows your account email address on the Settings screen of your own device. It is not sent to us.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of NIT Scanner shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Premium subscribers do not see ads.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • the Android advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • information about the ads shown and how you interact with them;
  • diagnostic information such as crash and performance data.

Google may use this information to show personalised ads, depending on your settings and, where the law requires it, your consent. You can read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings > "Ad privacy choices".
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • You can manage ad personalisation for your Google account at adssettings.google.com.

7. Subscriptions and Purchases

NIT Scanner offers an optional Premium subscription. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your card number or other payment details.

Google Play tells the App only whether a Premium purchase is active. This status is stored on your device so the App can remove ads and unlock Premium features.

You can view or cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play (play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions).

8. Google Drive Backup (Optional)

Premium users can choose to turn on cloud backup. This feature is off unless you sign in with your Google account in the App's Settings screen.

When backup is on, the page images of the documents you scan are uploaded to your own Google Drive, in a folder named "NIT_Scanner_Backups". We do not receive a copy.

The App asks only for permission to create and manage the files it creates itself. It cannot see or change any other files in your Google Drive.

You can stop backups at any time by tapping "Sign Out" in the App's Settings. Files already uploaded stay in your Google Drive until you delete them there.

NIT Scanner's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Deleting a document or a page in the App removes it from the App's storage on your device.
  • Uninstalling the App, or clearing its data in your phone settings, removes all scans stored inside the App.
  • Files you exported to your Pictures or Downloads folder, shared with other apps, or backed up to Google Drive are outside the App and stay there until you delete them yourself.

10. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may process information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google ML Kit (scanning and text recognition)
  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google Play Billing (subscriptions)
  • Google Sign-In and Google Drive (optional backup)
  • Google Play services

Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

12. Children's Privacy

NIT Scanner is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

14. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or NIT Scanner, please contact us at nitxy07@gmail.com.
